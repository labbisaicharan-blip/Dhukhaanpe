How DhukaanPe Operates at 0% MDR

NPCI P2PM Tier • Legally Compliant & Sustainable

Government & NPCI Mandate

Under MeitY and NPCI directives, Peer-to-Merchant (P2PM) transactions for neighborhood retailers (MCC 5411 Grocery) carry zero merchant discount rate. Transactions ≤ ₹2,000 are completely free of interchange cuts. For transactions above ₹2,000, our Smart Bill Splitter enables splitting into sub-₹2,000 parts to preserve 0% MDR status.

Sustainable Business Model

Payment acceptance is completely free. We generate revenue through optional value-added merchant solutions:

  • 4G Speaker Hardware: ₹99–₹129/month SIM & maintenance subscription.
  • Vyapar Micro-Loans: Interest spread & origination commissions via daily 10% split settlements with partner NBFCs.
  • B2B FMCG Distributor Payouts: Direct digital stockist invoicing fee charged to distributors.
